Are these models Excel templates?

Yes. The catalog consists of Excel financial modeling templates designed to be customized for a specific company, transaction, or project.

Do the models use macros?

No. FinModelAI models are built without macros, keeping the workbook logic visible and easier to review.

Who are the models designed for?

They support analysts, finance teams, SaaS operators, solar and storage developers, hotel and rental property investors, and individuals comparing debt payoff strategies.

Can I change the assumptions and structure?

Yes. The models use color-coded inputs and industry-standard formatting and are designed to be easy to customize.
